# Swift packages

The standalone server links Apple's SwiftNIO, Swift Atomics, Swift Collections,
and Swift System packages. Versions and revisions are pinned in
`Package.resolved`. Their licenses and SwiftNIO's notices, including the vendored
llhttp and SHA-1 licenses, are included in the built app under
`Contents/Resources/Licenses`. The cpp_magic notice follows.
